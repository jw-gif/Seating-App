？ How it works

Placing & rearranging a group

Add groups in the Roster. To seat one: pick the tool Select, drag/click seats on the map, then click Seat on the group (any shape works — it clears the old placement first). Or click ⚡ Auto to auto-place a group, or ✨ Auto-arrange to seat everyone.

Selecting seats

Drag a marquee box = replace selection · hold Shift/Cmd = add · hold Alt = remove (box turns red) · a small click = toggle one seat. Click a section number to toggle that whole section; click a row letter in the gutter to toggle that whole row. Esc clears.

Move tool

Switch to Move group, then drag a colored block. Default keeps the exact shape (green solid = OK). Hold Shift (or if the shape doesn't fit) to reflow/reshape into the target (green dashed). Red = invalid. Locked groups can't be dragged.

Priority & off

Select seats, then Priority (amber ring) / High priority (red ring) / Clear priority. Toggle off (kill) makes seats unusable (grayed). Click a group's priority badge to cycle it.

Sessions & export

Name a setup and Save to snapshot everything; Load/Delete from the list. The session name is also the export title. ⤓ List downloads a clean text roster with seat ranges.

Logo theming

Click the logo slot to upload an image — the app extracts a brand accent color and re-themes itself. Hover the slot and click ✕ to remove and restore defaults.

Keyboard

⌘Z/Ctrl+Z undo · ⌘⇧Z/Ctrl+Y redo · Esc clear selection / close.
